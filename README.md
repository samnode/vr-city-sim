# VR City Sim

A SimCity-style city builder built for Meta Quest VR. Point, place, and grow your tiny city in 3D.

🎮 **[Play it live →](https://samnode.github.io/vr-city-sim/)**

## How to play

- Point your controller at the grid and pull the trigger to place buildings
- Cycle through building types (residential, commercial, roads, parks, and more)
- Watch your city stats update in real time

## Run locally

Just open `index.html` in a browser — no build step needed.
